It watched the trends, read the numbers, made the content, handled the comments, and answered the reviews. Then it tells you what it did, what it decided, and the few things it needs from you. "Morning. Two trends broke in your market overnight, so I built the posts. Your best content this week was Tuesday's reel, so I made three more like it. What Marlowe does

Section label: THE WHOLE DEPARTMENT Heading: One partner. Every discipline. Intro: Marlowe is not one tool. It is a full marketing team, working together, behind one calm voice. The list (as a grid): Strategy and brand. Content and copy. Social media, posted for you. Comments and DMs, answered in your voice. Google Business and reviews. SEO and AI search. Design and creative. Short-form video. Competitor and trend research. Reporting that actually explains itself. Ads and email, as you grow.

How Marlowe is different

Section label: NOT ANOTHER AI TOOL Heading: Most AI waits to be told. Marlowe decides. Three points:

Proactive, not prompted. Other tools sit in a chat waiting for instructions. Marlowe works on its own and comes to you with it done.

It becomes your brand. Marlowe learns your voice, your market and what works for you, and gets sharper every week. It is your marketing, not generic output.

Agency expertise, built in. Marlowe carries Why Media's fifteen years of marketing knowledge. You are not renting software, you are hiring a team. Reassurance line: And you are always in control. Marlowe asks before anything that touches money, goes public, or matters.

Three steps

Section label: UP AND RUNNING IN MINUTES Heading: Three steps. Then it runs.

Connect. Point Marlowe at your website and channels.

Meet your brand. Marlowe studies everything and comes back with what it sees and what it would do first.

Set the dial. Choose how much Marlowe can do on its own. Then the morning briefs begin.
