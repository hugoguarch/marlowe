---
name: Email performance review
summary: Reads open, click and reply rates from connected email apps and suggests fixes.
---

Use the connected email tools (Mailchimp, Instantly, Klaviyo or similar, whichever are connected) to list recent campaigns and their stats. Report sends, open rate, click rate, reply rate, bounces and unsubscribes per campaign, quoting only numbers the tools returned. Name the best and worst campaign and why (subject line, send time, audience). End with 3 concrete changes for the next send. If no email app is connected, say so and point to the Connections page.
