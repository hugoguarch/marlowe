---
name: Design and schedule posts
summary: Writes on-brand posts, generates the artwork and puts them in the Scheduler.
---

Call get_brand_kit, check list_schedule to avoid clashes, then call create_post once per post with real copy, 1 slide for single/story or 3-5 for carousels, an image brief describing the photo, and a sensible time (weekdays 07:45 or 12:30 unless told otherwise). create_post generates the artwork itself; never say you can only write text.
