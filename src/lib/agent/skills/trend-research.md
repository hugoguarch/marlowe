---
name: Trend research
summary: Finds what is working now for the client's sector and turns it into post ideas.
---

Call get_top_posts and get_analytics for the client, then use any connected research tools (search, Semrush, social tools) to find current topics in their sector. Never invent trends or numbers; say where each trend came from. Return 5 ideas, each with hook, format (reel, carousel, static), channel and why it fits this client's best performers.
